💬 Chat Tab - Statistics Assistant

The chat lets you describe your scientific question in your own words. The assistant translates it into a statistical model, runs the analyses and explains the results in plain language.

Step 1: Load your dataset in the Data tab.
Step 2: Ask your question, e.g. "Does chilling affect the CO2 uptake of the plants?"
Step 3: Check the results in the results list. Every step of the assistant (formula, tests, diagnostic plots) is added there and to the history, exactly as if you had used the other tabs.

🎓 Teach mode

Select Teach me to learn the analysis instead of getting it done. The assistant only reads your data, formula and results and guides you with questions and hints; you run every step yourself. When you have created a result, ask the assistant for feedback on it. Switching the mode starts a new conversation.

🛠 Notes

  • The assistant can only act through the analyses of OpenStats; it cannot change your data.
  • Click a tool name in the chat (e.g. t_test) to see the exact call and the numbers the assistant received.
  • Language models can misinterpret results. Always compare the explanation with the results list.
  • Clear chat starts a new conversation; the results are kept.

⚙️ Setup

The chat is only available when a language model is configured, e.g. options(OpenStats.llm = "ollama/qwen3:4b-instruct") for a local model via Ollama, or options(OpenStats.llm = "anthropic/claude-opus-5-5") together with the environment variable ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. With an external provider, your data are sent to that provider.
